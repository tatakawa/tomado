How do I start a pomodoro?

Tap + on the left to add a task, tap the task to select it, then press play.

Can I change the focus or break length?

Yes. Open Settings (gear icon, bottom left). Focus can be 1–120 minutes and break 1–60 minutes.

Why didn't I hear the alert?

When the app is in the background or the phone is locked, alerts come as notifications. Make sure notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings → TomaDo, and check your silent switch and Focus mode.

How do I edit or delete a task?

Swipe left on the task. Deleting a task also removes its focus history.

Is my data backed up?

Your data is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all tasks and history.

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 11, 2026

TomaDo does not collect, upload or share any personal data.

  • Your tasks and focus history are stored only on your device. The developer cannot see them.
  • No account, no ads, and no analytics or third-party tracking.
  • Notifications are local and only used to tell you when a pomodoro or break ends.
  • Deleting a task deletes its focus history. Deleting the app removes all data.

怎么开始一个番茄？

点左边的 ＋ 添加任务，点一下任务选中它，再按播放键。

可以改番茄和休息的时长吗？

可以。打开左下角的设置，专注可设 1–120 分钟，休息可设 1–60 分钟。

为什么没听到提示音？

App 在后台或锁屏时，提醒以系统通知的方式出现。请在「设置 → TomaDo」里允许通知，并检查静音键和专注模式。

怎么修改或删除任务？

在任务上向左滑。删除任务会同时删除它的专注记录。

数据会备份吗？

数据只保存在你的设备上。删除 App 会清除全部任务和记录。

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 11 日

TomaDo 不收集、不上传、不分享任何个人数据。

  • 你创建的任务和专注记录只保存在你自己的设备上，开发者无法查看。
  • App 不需要注册账号，不包含广告，不使用任何统计分析或第三方追踪工具。
  • 通知权限只用于在番茄或休息结束时提醒你，全部是设备本地通知。
  • 删除任务会同时删除它的专注记录；删除 App 会清除全部数据。
